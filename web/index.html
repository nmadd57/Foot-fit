🦶 Foot Fit

Measure your foot with your phone

Print the marker template, put your foot on it and walk the camera around. Foot Fit tracks the paper, measures length and width to the millimetre and builds a 3D model you can download.

  1. 1 Print the template

    Open printable template ↗

    Print at 100% / Actual size. Then measure the ruler bar under the heel line:

  2. 2 Scan

    • Lay the sheet flat on a floor that contrasts with white paper.
    • Bare foot or a coloured sock. Back of the heel on the heel line.
    • Even, diffuse light. Avoid one strong lamp that casts hard shadows.
    • Keep your shin roughly vertical. A helper holding the phone makes it easier.

  3. 3 Results

    Length, ball width, heel width, instep height and girths, a 3D model (STL/OBJ) and — if an AI backend is connected — a shoe-fit report.

Images are processed in your browser. When you use an AI feature, a few downscaled images and your measurements are sent to the configured AI endpoint.
